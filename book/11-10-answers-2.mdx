## 9. Passive with two objects, the answers — answered

1. was given the prize
2. was told the news
3. was sent the letter
4. was offered a job
5. were shown the results

Note the pattern: where the verb permits it, the **person** becomes the
subject (*was given the prize*). *Tell* is the exception and will not
allow *the news was told her*.

## 10. The perfect — answered

4. **lost** — found again, closed

7. **have never seen**

9. **had already eaten** — earlier than the arrival

9. waiting

## 12. Auxiliaries — answered

1. He **can** swim very well.
2. She must **not** go now, or She **has** to go now.
3. I **cannot** help you.
4. He does **not** work on Sundays.
5. Never **had I** seen such a thing.
6. Hardly had I arrived **when** the phone rang.
7. She **had had** a car since she was eighteen. — *has had ... has been*
   mixes the perfect with the simple; the second should be *was*
8. The report **was written** yesterday.
9. I **will have finished** it by Friday.
10. They **do not** know the answer.

## 13. The *-ed* ending — answered

| verb | form | sound | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. work | worked | /t/ | voiceless, and the base ends in /k/ |
| 2. need | needed | /ɪd/ | the base already ends in /d/ |
| 3. play | played | /d/ | a vowel before it |
| 4. laugh | laughed | /t/ | voiceless /f/ |
| 5. study | studied | /d/ | a vowel before it |
| 6. open | opened | /d/ | voiced /n/ |
| 7. wash | washed | /t/ | voiceless /ʃ/ |
| 8. call | called | /d/ | voiced /l/ |
| 9. count | counted | /ɪd/ | the base already ends in /t/ |
| 10. visit | visited | /ɪd/ | the base already ends in /t/ |

Every one of these is **two syllables**, and the stress is on the first.

## 15. Reduced relatives — answered

1. the man **standing** there
2. the road **leading** to town
3. the question **asked** by the interviewer
4. the students **taking** the exam
5. the report **written** in 2019
6. the photos **taken** last year
7. the book **bought** yesterday
8. the people **living** next door
9. the damage **caused** by the storm
10. the woman **writing** the letter

Numbers 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 are **passive**, so they take *-ed*. The others are
**active**, so they take *-ing*. That is the rule, and 7 is the one to get
right: *bought* is active here, because the book was doing the buying,
which is impossible, and which is why "the book buying by me" would be
obscene.

## 16. Fix the errors — answered

7. I **saw** him yesterday at the party.
8. She suggested **meeting** at eight.
9. The book **was written** in 1990.
10. There **are** many people in the room.

## 17. Write ten sentences — answered

Model answers.

1. A present simple habit: *I **get up** at seven every day.*
2. A present continuous: *I **am reading** your email right now.*
3. A present perfect with *for*: *I **have worked** here **for** six
   years.*
4. A present perfect with *since*: *I **have worked** here **since** 2019.*
5. A past simple event: *I **wrote** the letter on Monday.*
6. A past continuous interrupted: *I **was reading** when the phone
   **rang**.*
7. A future with *will*: *I **will call** you when I get home.*
8. A future with *going to*: *I **am going to** study medicine.*
9. A present simple passive: *The room **is cleaned** every day.*
10. A passive with a modal: *The report **must be finished** by Friday.*
