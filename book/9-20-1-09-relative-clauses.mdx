# 09 Relative Clauses

**From the deck:** Defining

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The woman ​ is standing there is my aunt. · Completa: identifies the woman | who |

A defining relative clause identifies the noun. It takes NO commas.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The woman ​ is standing there is my aunt. · Escribe: identifies the woman | who |

(3 car.)  ·  who

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The house ​ I grew up is for sale. · Completa: the house | that |

that = people and things. It is far more common than most learners realise: it is the most frequent relative pronoun.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The house ​ I grew up is for sale. · Escribe: the house | that |

(4 car.)  ·  that

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| This is the reason ​ I left. · Completa: the reason | why |

why replaces the reason that / for which. It is used after abstract nouns such as reason.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| This is the reason ​ I left. · Escribe: the reason | why |

(3 car.)  ·  why

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I still remember the day ​ we met. · Completa: the day (a time) | when |

when = an adverb of time in defining clauses. Equivalent to on which / in which.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I still remember the day ​ we met. · Escribe: the day (a time) | when |

(4 car.)  ·  when

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He failed the exam ​ he worked too hard. · Completa: reason | because |

Relative adverbs of reason: because, since, as. They are mainly used in non-defining clauses.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He failed the exam ​ he worked too hard. · Escribe: reason | because |

(7 car.)  ·  because

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| My sister, ​ lives in Lima, is a doctor. · Completa: with commas = extra information | who |

A non-defining clause (with commas) adds extra information. It practically only allows who / which / whose (and where / when in standard English).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| My sister, ​ lives in Lima, is a doctor. · Escribe: with commas = extra information | who |

(3 car.)  ·  who

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I bought a jacket, ​ was very cheap. · Completa: the jacket (a thing) | which |

which for things, always in non-defining clauses.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I bought a jacket, ​ was very cheap. · Escribe: the jacket (a thing) | which |

(5 car.)  ·  which

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I don't have a car, ​ means I use the bus. · Completa: a whole clause | which |

Cataphora: which stands for an entire clause.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I don't have a car, ​ means I use the bus. · Escribe: a whole clause | which |

(5 car.)  ·  which

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| That's the house ​ I grew up. · Completa: the house (a place) | where |

where = in which / at which (place). It can be replaced by in which.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| That's the house ​ I grew up. · Escribe: the house (a place) | where |

(5 car.)  ·  where

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'm not sure ​ he will come. · Completa: whether | if/whether |

Noun clauses work as subject, object or complement. If / whether for 'whether'.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'm not sure ​ he will come. · Escribe: whether | if/whether |

(10 car.)  ·  if/whether

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The problem ​ is that we don't have time. · Completa: the problem is that | is |

that introduces subject, object and complement clauses. That can be omitted.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The problem ​ is that we don't have time. · Escribe: the problem is that | is |

(2 car.)  ·  is

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| It surprised me ​ he had already left. · Completa: it surprised me that | that |

It + verb + that + clause.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| It surprised me ​ he had already left. · Escribe: it surprised me that | that |

(4 car.)  ·  that

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She insisted ​ the bill was wrong. · Completa: insisted that | that |

insist / suggest / demand / recommend + that + clause. With many of these verbs, should is used in the subjunctive.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She insisted ​ the bill was wrong. · Escribe: insisted that | that |

(4 car.)  ·  that

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| This is the book ​ I told you about. · Completa: replaces 'which' | that |

that / which / it (informal) can replace the relative pronoun when it is the object.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| This is the book ​ I told you about. · Escribe: replaces 'which' | that |

(4 car.)  ·  that

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The woman ​ car was stolen called the police. · Completa: whose | whose |

whose = 'whose' (possession). It works with people and things.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The woman ​ car was stolen called the police. · Escribe: whose | whose |

(5 car.)  ·  whose

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The man ​ I met yesterday is from Peru. · Completa: whom (formal object) | whom |

whom = 'whom' (object, formal). who can also be an object in current usage.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The man ​ I met yesterday is from Peru. · Escribe: whom (formal object) | whom |

(4 car.)  ·  whom

The woman {{c1::whose}} car was stolen called the police.

**Answer:** whose

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The project ​ we're working on is nearly done. · Completa: the project (with a preposition) | which |

When the relative takes a preposition, only which is possible (or who/whom for people). NEVER that.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The project ​ we're working on is nearly done. · Escribe: the project (with a preposition) | which |

(5 car.)  ·  which

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She had a reason ​ she didn't tell anyone. · Completa: the reason (with a preposition) | for which |

With an obligatory preposition, the relative must be which, who (people) or whom.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She had a reason ​ she didn't tell anyone. · Escribe: the reason (with a preposition) | for which |

(9 car.)  ·  for which

The project {{c1::which}} we're working on is nearly done.

**Answer:** which
