# 15 Common Errors

## B2-C1 Errors 15 Common Errors (1/1), block 1

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| If I ​ you, I'd take the job. · Completa: if I were you | were |

ERROR: 'if I would be you'. In English, if + were for unreal hypotheses, with all persons.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| If I ​ you, I'd take the job. · Escribe: if I were you | were |

(4 car.)  ·  were

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I suggest you ​ a doctor. · Completa: suggest you see | see |

ERROR: suggest you to see. Suggestion verbs (suggest, recommend, advise, propose, insist) take that + clause or + V-ing.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I suggest you ​ a doctor. · Escribe: suggest you see | see |

(3 car.)  ·  see

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The meeting was ​ by the boss. · Completa: was postponed | postponed |

ERROR: a wrong passive. In English: postpone = be postponed / be put off.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The meeting was ​ by the boss. · Escribe: was postponed | postponed |

(9 car.)  ·  postponed

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's good ​ playing the piano. · Completa: good at playing | at |

ERROR: 'be good at + V'. English: good at + V-ing or good at + noun.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's good ​ playing the piano. · Escribe: good at playing | at |

(2 car.)  ·  at

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He denied ​ the money. · Completa: denied stealing | stealing |

ERROR: 'denied to steal'. Correct: deny + V-ing (past) or deny + to + base (future).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He denied ​ the money. · Escribe: denied stealing | stealing |

(8 car.)  ·  stealing

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She apologised ​ breaking the vase. · Completa: apologised for | for |

ERROR: apologise to breaking. Correct: apologise for + V-ing.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She apologised ​ breaking the vase. · Escribe: apologised for | for |

(3 car.)  ·  for

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I need to ​ about the exam results. · Completa: find out about | find out |

ERROR: 'know about'. For 'to find out': find out, hear about, learn about, be told, come to know.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I need to ​ about the exam results. · Escribe: find out about | find out |

(8 car.)  ·  find out

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'm looking forward ​ you. · Completa: to seeing | to seeing |

ERROR: look forward to see you. The to is a preposition, so it goes with -ing.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'm looking forward ​ you. · Escribe: to seeing | to seeing |

(9 car.)  ·  to seeing

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The book ​ by a famous writer. · Completa: was written | was written |

ERROR: a badly formed passive. Always be + participle.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The book ​ by a famous writer. · Escribe: was written | was written |

(11 car.)  ·  was written

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'd rather you ​ smoke in front of me. · Completa: would rather you didn't | didn't |

ERROR: would rather you not to smoke. Would rather + subject + Past Simple for someone else's past; would rather not + base for yourself.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'd rather you ​ smoke in front of me. · Escribe: would rather you didn't | didn't |

(6 car.)  ·  didn't

ERROR: Do you like going...? Do you like going when asking about an ACTIVITY. Correct: Do you like going? / Do you like to go? / Do you enjoy going? Use to when asking for an OPINION and -ing when asking about the ACTIVITY.

| cue | answer |

(2 car.)  ·  Do

| cue | answer |
|---|---|

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I ​ to London three times. · Escribe: have been | have been |

(9 car.)  ·  have been

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| There ​ a lot of people at the concert. · Completa: there were | were |

ERROR: there had. In English: there is / there are / there was / there were. With the Past Simple: there was / there were.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|

(4 car.)  ·  were

| cue | answer |
|---|---|

ERROR: 'a good advice' / 'good advices'. Advice is UNCOUNTABLE: some advice, a piece of advice, much advice. NEVER advices or an advice.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He gave me ​ good advice. · Escribe: some good advice | some |

(4 car.)  ·  some

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's taller ​ her brother. · Completa: taller than | than |

ERROR: 'more taller'. In English, than for comparatives and equality; as...as for 'as...as'.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's taller ​ her brother. · Escribe: taller than | than |

(4 car.)  ·  than

| cue | answer |
|---|---|

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I ​ to bed at 11 every night. · Escribe: go to bed | go |
