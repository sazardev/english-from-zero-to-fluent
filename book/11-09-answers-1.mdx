# Answers: foundations

Answers to 11.1. Where a question has more than one correct answer, the
first is given and the alternative is noted.

## 1. Articles — answered

1. **a** teacher
2. **the** hospital near **the** river
3. **a** book. **The** book was about English history.
4. **the** best student in **the** class
5. **an** hour — *an* before a vowel *sound*
6. **an** interesting film
7. no article — football, tennis and chess take none
8. **a** small village in **the** north of **the** country
9. **The** sun rises in **the** east. Both are unique.
10. **an** honest man — the *h* is silent

## 2. Plurals and countability — answered

Countable versions:

11. a piece of advice / two pieces of advice

13. a slice of bread / two loaves of bread
14. a piece of furniture / three items of furniture

## 3. Pronouns and determiners — answered

1. **It** can be read in an hour.
2. **theirs** — and *my car is faster than theirs* compares a car with
   cars, not one car with another
3. **everyone** has gone. *No one* and *nobody* would also be correct.
4. **they**
5. **its**
6. **It was** — the *it*-cleft from part 5
7. **their** own desk
8. **right** — *neither of the answers was right*
9. **reliable** or *significant* or any adjective that fits. The grammar is
   what is being tested, not the meaning.
10. **done** — *much of the work is done*

## 7. What the *there* refers to — answered

1. the engine
2. the wall
3. the fridge
4. nothing — the situation
5. the building
6. the delay
7. the drug

In 4 and 7 the *there* is not pointing at anything, which is what makes
those two different from the others.

## 8. Fix the errors — answered

1. She **writes** every day without exception.
2. I **saw** that film last year and it was terrible.
3. **It's** important to arrive on time.
4. The dog wagged **its** tail.
5. He depends **on** his brother for everything.
6. We discussed **the** problem for an hour.
7. There **are** two reasons for the delay.
8. After I **left**, she shut the door. — *after* already fixes the order
9. Although the film was long, I enjoyed it.
10. The keys are in the pocket of **its** bag.

## 9. Write five sentences — answered

Model answers.

1. **A** countable noun: *I bought **a** book about verbs **yesterday**.*
2. An uncountable noun: *I need **some** information about verbs.*
3. There as existential: ***There is** a book about verbs on the table.*
4. It as placeholder: ***It is** important to learn verbs **in context**.*
5. A non-defining relative clause: *My teacher, **who lives in
   Manchester**, is very patient.*

## 10. Correct this paragraph — answered

> **The information is** very important for **understanding** the article.
> **There are** a lot of people who **do not know** the rules about *a*,
> *an* and *the*. If you **write** it **badly**, the reader can see it
> immediately. **I read** this book last year and I **liked it** very much.
> **It is** the best book about English that I **have** ever read.

The errors, in the order of the chapter: the uncountable noun
(*informations*), the gerund after a preposition, the existential *there*
with a plural, the third person *-s* after a negative *do not*, the
present simple where a past is meant, an adverb where an adjective
belongs, the missing object after *liked*, the *its* against *it's*, and
the past perfect where a present perfect is needed.
