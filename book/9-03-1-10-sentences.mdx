# 10 Sentences

## Adverbs 10 Sentences (1/1), block 1

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I only found out ​ . · Completa: only + yesterday | yesterday |

Focus adverbs (only, just, even, still, already, also, too, never, not) go before the main verb.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I only found out ​ . · Escribe: only + yesterday | yesterday |

(9 car.)  ·  yesterday

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She was so tired ​ she couldn't keep her eyes open. · Completa: so... that | that |

so + adjective + that + result (or too + adjective + to + base).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She was so tired ​ she couldn't keep her eyes open. · Escribe: so... that | that |

(4 car.)  ·  that

Where each adverb goes (word order is not free) · B1

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| It was in the office ​ I left my keys. · Completa: it was in the office that | that |

It cleft = It + be + element + that/who + the rest. It exists to focus on one piece of information.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| It was in the office ​ I left my keys. · Escribe: it was in the office that | that |

(4 car.)  ·  that

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| What I need ​ a break. · Completa: what I need is | is |

Wh-cleft: What + clause + verb. Extremely useful to focus, correct and summarise.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| What I need ​ a break. · Escribe: what I need is | is |

(2 car.)  ·  is

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| It wasn't until 3pm ​ the meeting started. · Completa: not until | that |

With until/before/only use the construction It + be + adverb + that (no inversion).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| It wasn't until 3pm ​ the meeting started. · Escribe: not until | that |

(4 car.)  ·  that

It was Ana {{c1::that}} called me.

**Answer:** that

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ being poor, she never complained. · Completa: despite being poor | Despite |

Despite / In spite of / Even though.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ being poor, she never complained. · Escribe: despite being poor | Despite |

(7 car.)  ·  Despite

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The film was boring. ​, I enjoyed it. · Completa: however | However |

Contrast linkers: however, although, though, whereas, nevertheless, on the other hand, yet, still, in contrast.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The film was boring. ​, I enjoyed it. · Escribe: however | However |

(7 car.)  ·  However

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He gets ​ , ​ he never seems tired. · Completa: two gaps: on / yet | on, yet |

get / carry on + V-ing = keep. Yet = however.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He gets ​ , ​ he never seems tired. · Escribe: two gaps: on / yet | on, yet |

(7 car.)  ·  on, yet

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| Never ​ I seen such a mess. · Completa: negative adverb first | have |

If a negative adverb (never, rarely, seldom, not only, hardly, no sooner, little, nowhere) opens the clause, the order is inverted: auxiliary + subject + verb.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| Never ​ I seen such a mess. · Escribe: negative adverb first | have |

(4 car.)  ·  have I

## Inversion 10 Sentences (1/1), block 2

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| So ​ the news that nobody spoke. · Completa: so ... that | was |

So + adjective + Auxiliary + subject + verb. If the clause has only or just, that is used and there is no inversion.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| So ​ the news that nobody spoke. · Escribe: so ... that | was |

(3 car.)  ·  was the news

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
