# Your first week, hour by hour

Week 1 of the programme in more detail, because the first week is the one
that is done or not done, and it is the one where most people need to be
told exactly what to do rather than what to practise.

Total time: about five hours across seven days. That is not a lot, and
that is the point. A first week of two hours a day does not happen twice.

## Day 1 — set up the tools

Nothing to read. Ninety minutes, one sitting.

| minutes | what |
|---|---|
| 15 | install a dictionary and find where the **examples** are, not just the definitions |
| 15 | create the deck, and add the first 20 cards by hand |
| 10 | create the correction log, an empty file, named after today |
| 10 | install a recorder and make a 60-second recording of yourself reading anything |
| 20 | read 0.3, your tools, in full |
| 20 | read 12.0, this track's opening chapter |

**Done when:** you can record yourself and play it back, and the log file
exists.

The 20 cards you add by hand today are the ones you got wrong this week.
If you have not got any yet, add the 20 hardest words in this chapter. The
point is not the words, it is that you have added them by hand, because a
card you typed yourself is a card you will review.

## Day 2 — the method, and your first text

| minutes | what |
|---|---|
| 10 | review: only yesterday's cards |
| 15 | read 0.1, how to use this book, and 0.2, the method |
| 15 | **write 100 words: what you did yesterday.** Past tense only. |
| 10 | correct it: read aloud, look for your five errors, check every verb |
| 10 | write every error in the log |
| 20 | listen: any English audio with subtitles, delayed |

**Done when:** 100 words exist, in the past tense, corrected, and the log
has entries.

The correction step is the one that will be skipped. Do not skip it. The
writing is worthless without the correction, and you will not feel that
now, which is exactly why the programme tells you to do it rather than
leaving it to judgement.

## Day 3 — the levels, and a baseline

| minutes | what |
|---|---|
| 10 | review |
| 15 | read 0.4, the levels |
| 10 | take Test 1 in 11.5, ten minutes, closed book, timed |
| 10 | read the answer key, 11.9, and mark it honestly |
| 10 | write 100 words: what your English is for, and why you chose this book |
| 15 | add ten cards from the errors you just found |

**Done when:** you have a number for Test 1 and it is written down.

Write the number down. In week 12 you will take the same test and the
difference between the two numbers is the only honest measure of what
twelve weeks did.

## Day 5 — articles

| minutes | what |
|---|---|
| 10 | review |
| 15 | read 1.3, articles, in full |
| 15 | **write 100 words: the three things on your desk, and where they are** |
| 10 | correct and log |
| 10 | do 11.1 §1, articles, and check in 11.9 |
| 10 | listen |

Articles are the first genuinely hard thing in the book and they are
everywhere. Expect to get half of §1 wrong the first time. That is
correct, and the answer key is two pages away in 11.9.

## Day 7 — review the week

| minutes | what |
|---|---|
| 10 | review |
| 20 | **re-read everything you wrote this week and correct it again** |
| 15 | do 11.1 §8, fix the errors, and §10, the paragraph |
| 20 | check 11.9 for both |
| 10 | write 100 words on anything. Do not stop to look anything up |
| 15 | record yourself reading your week-1 writing aloud, for two minutes |

**Done when:** you have seven days in the log, one recording, one number.

The second correction pass on day 7 is the single most useful hour of the
week. Errors you made on day 2 and did not see are still there on day 7,
and the count of them is the real measure of your first week.

<Warning>
The two ways week 1 goes wrong, both common, both avoidable.

**You skip the writing because the grammar is more interesting.** The
grammar is more interesting. Write anyway. A 100-word text with ten
article errors, corrected, teaches you more about articles than forty pages
about them, because the ten errors are yours.

**You add too many cards on day 1.** Fifty feels productive. Twenty is
enough for week 1, and the difference is whether you are still reviewing
on day 7. A deck you have stopped reviewing is worse than a small deck you
have not, because the first one tells you the method does not work.

The rule for cards, which holds for the whole book: **add cards only from
the log, never from the dictionary.** A card from a word you met while
reading is a card for a failure you have not had yet.
</Warning>

## What the end of week 1 should look like

| check | expected |
|---|---|
| days in the log | 7 of 7 |
| words written | 600 to 700, corrected |
| cards added | 20 to 40, all from the log |
| Test 1 score | written down, and you know what it means |
| recordings | 2, and you have heard both |
| errors in the log | 20 to 40, each with the rule in one line |

If any row is zero except the last, week 1 worked. If the log is empty,
the programme is not running, and the rest of it will not either.

## What week 2 needs from you

Nothing new. You need the same seven days again, with articles and
pronouns becoming automatic rather than correct, and part 1.5 replacing
1.3 on the reading list.
