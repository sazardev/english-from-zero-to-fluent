# 23 Exam Traps

**From the deck:** The Errors That Cost the Most Marks

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I've lived here ​ 2015. · Completa: since 2015 | since |

Since + a point, for + a duration. This is error #1 at B2.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I've lived here ​ 2015. · Escribe: since 2015 | since |

(5 car.)  ·  since

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| If I ​ more time, I'd travel. · Completa: had more time | had |

The second conditional in the negative and with wish uses the past simple (or were). NEVER will.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| If I ​ more time, I'd travel. · Escribe: had more time | had |

(3 car.)  ·  had

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'd rather you ​ smoke here. · Completa: didn't smoke | didn't |

Would rather + subject + Past Simple for the past. Would rather + base for yourself.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'd rather you ​ smoke here. · Escribe: didn't smoke | didn't |

(6 car.)  ·  didn't

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The report needs ​ before Friday. · Completa: to be checked | to be checked |

need / must / should + be + V3 for the passive. Also needs checking (without to be).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The report needs ​ before Friday. · Escribe: to be checked | to be checked |

(13 car.)  ·  to be checked

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'm looking forward ​ the results. · Completa: to the results | to |

look forward to + V-ing. The to is a preposition. be looking forward to hearing from you.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'm looking forward ​ the results. · Escribe: to the results | to |

(2 car.)  ·  to

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She suggested ​ the meeting. · Completa: postponing | postponing |

suggest / recommend / advise / propose / insist + V-ing or + that + clause. Never to.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She suggested ​ the meeting. · Escribe: postponing | postponing |

(10 car.)  ·  postponing

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ he is rich, he isn't happy. · Completa: although | Although |

Although / though / even though + clause. Despite / in spite of + -ing / noun.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ he is rich, he isn't happy. · Escribe: although | Although |

(8 car.)  ·  Although

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| There ​ been a lot of changes. · Completa: there has been | has |

There has been (singular) when the real subject is 'change' or the fact itself. NEVER there have been changes with that meaning.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| There ​ been a lot of changes. · Escribe: there has been | has |

(3 car.)  ·  has

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| Neither of the answers ​ correct. · Completa: is | is |

Neither of / either of / none of + of → singular verb (BrE) or plural (AmE). In an exam: singular.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| Neither of the answers ​ correct. · Escribe: is | is |

(2 car.)  ·  is

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The number of students ​ increasing. · Completa: is increasing | is |

The number of + plural → singular verb. A number of + plural → plural verb.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The number of students ​ increasing. · Escribe: is increasing | is |

(2 car.)  ·  is

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's the only person who ​ the answer. · Completa: knows | knows |

A non-defining clause with who takes a plural verb if who is plural. With the only one who + singular it is singular.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's the only person who ​ the answer. · Escribe: knows | knows |

(5 car.)  ·  knows

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He was arrested and charged ​ murder. · Completa: charged with | with |

charged with = accused of a crime. accused of = accused of. blamed for = blamed for.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He was arrested and charged ​ murder. · Escribe: charged with | with |

(4 car.)  ·  with

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I can't afford ​ a new car. · Completa: to buy | to buy |

can't afford + to + base or can't afford + noun. NEVER can't afford buying.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I can't afford ​ a new car. · Escribe: to buy | to buy |

(6 car.)  ·  to buy

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ matters is that we don't have time. · Completa: what matters is | What |

What + clause = 'what'. Mind the punctuation: What matters is (no comma).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ matters is that we don't have time. · Escribe: what matters is | What |

(4 car.)  ·  What

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's good ​ children. · Completa: good with | with |

good with (people) vs good at (skills). This error is worth marks in every exam.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's good ​ children. · Escribe: good with | with |

(4 car.)  ·  with

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I only found out ​ . · Completa: yesterday | yesterday |

Time adverbs at the end of the clause: yesterday, today, tomorrow, tonight, last night, next week, last year.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I only found out ​ . · Escribe: yesterday | yesterday |

(9 car.)  ·  yesterday

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ enough time, we'd have helped. · Completa: had we had | Had we had |

The third conditional can be inverted by fronting the auxiliary: Had we had enough time… (C1, elegant).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ enough time, we'd have helped. · Escribe: had we had | Had we had |

(10 car.)  ·  Had we had

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| It's ​ that we don't have enough staff. · Completa: it's clear that | clear |

It is + adj + that + clause: clear, obvious, essential, vital, important, likely, possible, surprising, unlikely. With for: It is difficult for someone TO….

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| It's ​ that we don't have enough staff. · Escribe: it's clear that | clear |

(5 car.)  ·  clear

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's the tallest woman ​ has ever won the prize. · Completa: to have ever | to |

the + superlative + person + to have + participle = the most ... that. A very typical B2 error.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's the tallest woman ​ has ever won the prize. · Escribe: to have ever | to |

(2 car.)  ·  to

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| How much ​ it cost? · Completa: does it cost | does |

Indirect questions: no inversion, statement word order. How much does it cost? / Could you tell me how much it costs?

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| How much ​ it cost? · Escribe: does it cost | does |

(4 car.)  ·  does

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'd prefer ​ the plane to the train. · Completa: taking | taking |

prefer / would prefer + V-ing / to + base. NEVER to + V after prefer (but YES with prefer A to B!).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'd prefer ​ the plane to the train. · Escribe: taking | taking |

(6 car.)  ·  taking

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The police ​ arrived yet. · Completa: haven't arrived yet | haven't |

In the present with yet you use the Present Perfect (haven't arrived yet). NEVER haven't arrived already.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The police ​ arrived yet. · Escribe: haven't arrived yet | haven't |

(7 car.)  ·  haven't

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| There's no point ​ about it. · Completa: in arguing | in arguing |

There is no point in + V-ing. There's no point to argue is WRONG.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| There's no point ​ about it. · Escribe: in arguing | in arguing |

(10 car.)  ·  in arguing

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's used to ​ in a big city. · Completa: living | living |

be used to + noun / V-ing = be used to. used to + base = used to. Never confuse them.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| She's used to ​ in a big city. · Escribe: living | living |

(6 car.)  ·  living

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'd rather you ​ the offer. · Completa: accepted | accepted |

would rather + past simple for inferences about someone else's past. would rather not + base for yourself.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I'd rather you ​ the offer. · Escribe: accepted | accepted |

(8 car.)  ·  accepted

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The more I practise, ​ I get. · Completa: the better | the better |

The + comparative, the + comparative. NEVER more better.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| The more I practise, ​ I get. · Escribe: the better | the better |

(10 car.)  ·  the better

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He apologised ​ breaking the promise. · Completa: for | for |

apologise for + V-ing / noun. apologise to someone (to a person).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He apologised ​ breaking the promise. · Escribe: for | for |

(3 car.)  ·  for

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ you have any questions, please let me know. · Completa: should you have | Should |

Should + subject + base = in case (formal inversion). C1 and very common in papers.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ you have any questions, please let me know. · Escribe: should you have | Should |

(6 car.)  ·  Should
