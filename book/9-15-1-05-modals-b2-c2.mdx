# 05 Modals (B2-C2)

## Certainty Scale 05 Modals (B2-C2) (1/1), block 1

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He ​ (be) at home. It's 11pm and his car is in the drive. · Completa: almost certain deduction | must be |

must = I'm 95% sure. may/might = 50%. could = possibly. can't = I'm sure it ISN'T.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He ​ (be) at home. It's 11pm and his car is in the drive. · Escribe: almost certain deduction | must be |
