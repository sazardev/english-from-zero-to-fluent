# 02 Verb System

## Modals: Basics 02 Verb System (1/1), block 1

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| You ​ smoke here. It's forbidden. · Completa: prohibition | must not |

must not / mustn't = PROHIBITION (an external rule). don't have to / doesn't have to = not necessary (you may, it's just not needed).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| You ​ smoke here. It's forbidden. · Escribe: prohibition | must not |

(8 car.)  ·  must not

|---|---|

(6 car.)  ·  should go

(4 car.)  ·  must be

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| Look! The baby ​ right now. · Completa: right now | is crying |

Present Continuous = am/is/are + V-ing. Used for actions in progress now, for temporary periods (this week, these days), and for near-future arrangements.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| Look! The baby ​ right now. · Escribe: right now | is crying |

(9 car.)  ·  is crying

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I ​ my car. I can't stop. · Completa: in progress, not interruptible | am repairing |

The continuous marks limited temporality: the action is happening in a defined period and will not go on.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I ​ my car. I can't stop. · Escribe: in progress, not interruptible | am repairing |

(12 car.)  ·  am repairing

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I ​ here since 2015. · Completa: stative verb (not continuous) | have lived |

Stative verbs (states, not actions) do not take the continuous in their literal sense. Use the Present Perfect or the Simple.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| I ​ here since 2015. · Escribe: stative verb (not continuous) | have lived |

(10 car.)  ·  have lived

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He ​ smoke. He gave it up last year. · Completa: past habit he no longer has | doesn't |

A past habit that still holds but can change: Present Simple negative. A temporary break uses the continuous: He's not smoking (anymore / at the moment).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| He ​ smoke. He gave it up last year. · Escribe: past habit he no longer has | doesn't |

(7 car.)  ·  doesn't smoke

## Present Simple 02 Verb System (1/1), block 2

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| Water ​ at 100 degrees Celsius. · Completa: 3rd person (habit / general truth) | boils |

Present Simple for habits, routines, general truths, scheduled future events. In the 3rd person (he/she/it) add -s / -es.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| Water ​ at 100 degrees Celsius. · Escribe: 3rd person (habit / general truth) | boils |

(5 car.)  ·  boils

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ he like sushi? · Completa: yes/no question | Does |

To form questions and negatives in the Present Simple, use the auxiliary do / does / did, and the main verb returns to its base form.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| ​ he like sushi? · Escribe: yes/no question | Does |

(4 car.)  ·  Does he like

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| How often ​ you go to the gym? · Completa: how often | do |

How often / How much / How long / What time questions in the present take do + base verb.

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| How often ​ you go to the gym? · Escribe: how often | do |

(2 car.)  ·  do you go

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| You look great, ​ ? · Completa: tag question, positive | don't you |

Tag questions use the auxiliary of the main verb: positive statement → negative tag (asking for confirmation), negative statement → positive tag (genuine doubt).

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| You look great, ​ ? · Escribe: tag question, positive | don't you |

(9 car.)  ·  don't you

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| How much ​ it cost? · Completa: subject question | does |

When the question has a subject (who, what, how much, how many), the main verb needs no auxiliary: How much does it cost?

| cue | answer |
|---|---|
| How much ​ it cost? · Escribe: subject question | does |

(4 car.)  ·  does it cost
